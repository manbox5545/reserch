01 / WHAT IS DEMOS?

Two frontiers.
One curiosity.

DEMOS is an exploration-focused community built around two connected areas of curiosity: understanding the mind and exploring observable space.

01 — THE MIND

Explore the inner universe

We explore questions about the mind, consciousness, awareness, perception, memory and the way people experience the world. We share questions, compare ideas and learn from research.

02 — OBSERVABLE SPACE

Explore the outer universe

We explore space, galaxies, stars, planets, cosmic phenomena and the science used to observe the universe. We look at discoveries, scientific explanations and unanswered questions.
